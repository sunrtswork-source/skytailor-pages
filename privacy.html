Privacy Policy

Skytailor Poster · last updated 6 November 2026

Skytailor Poster is an internal tool of SkyTailor Travel, operated under SKT Holiday (TAT licence 11/11191), Bangkok, Thailand. It connects only to our own TikTok account, @skytailortravel. It does not collect data about other TikTok users or the public.

What we receive from TikTok

  • user.info.basic / user.info.profile: our account's ID, display name and username, to check that the tool is connected to @skytailortravel and nothing else.
  • user.info.stats: our account's follower, following, like and video counts.
  • video.list: our own videos' IDs, titles, dates and view, like, comment and share counts.
  • video.upload: lets the tool send our own finished videos to our TikTok drafts. It does not publish; a team member publishes in TikTok.

How we use it

Only to put our own drafts into TikTok and to count how our own posts perform in our internal marketing report. We do not sell, rent or share this data, use it for advertising, or combine it with customer data.

Where it is kept

The TikTok login (refresh token) is kept encrypted in a private repository that only our team can open. Account and video counts are kept as daily totals in the same private repository. Data travels over HTTPS only.

How long

Daily counts are kept while we use them for reporting. The login is deleted when the tool is disconnected; TikTok's login expires by itself after at most one year.

Removing access and deleting data

Disconnect the tool at any time in TikTok: Settings and privacy › Security › Manage app permissions › Skytailor Poster › Remove access. To ask us to delete the stored data, contact us below and we will do it within 30 days.

Children

The tool is for our staff only and is not meant for anyone under 18.
