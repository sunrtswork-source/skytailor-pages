Skytailor Poster

An internal tool of SkyTailor Travel, Bangkok.

Skytailor Poster is used only by our own team for our own TikTok account, @skytailortravel. It does two things:

  • It sends videos our team has already finished and approved to our TikTok drafts (inbox). A team member then opens TikTok, checks the post, adds the caption and taps Post. Nothing is published without a person doing it in TikTok.
  • It reads our own account's public counts (followers, likes, video views) once a day for our internal marketing report.

It is not offered to other TikTok users.
